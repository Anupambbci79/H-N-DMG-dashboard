How the risk is worked out
  • PTH carries most weight. ATA 2018: PTH ≥ 15 pg/mL at ≥ 20 min after thyroidectomy makes intensive monitoring and supplementation unnecessary; < 15 pg/mL means increased risk. Below 10 pg/mL is treated here as high risk (International Task Force 2022).
  • Calcium. ATA 2018: consider oral calcium if serum Ca < 8.5 mg/dL or ionised Ca < 1.1 mmol/L. A rise between 6 and 12 h is reassuring; a fall, or corrected Ca < 8.0 mg/dL, raises the risk.
  • Operative factors move the estimate up one step when two or more are present.
  • Lobectomy alone is low risk.

Rule-based stratification from guideline thresholds, not a statistically validated model. Check doses against your local protocol and formulary.
